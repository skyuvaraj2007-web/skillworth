ETHICAL COGNITION

AI assists. People decide.

In high-stakes assessment, automated algorithms should never replace human judgment. SkillWorth uses deterministic AI strictly as a supportive co-pilot: parsing spoken vocabulary, mapping unstructured work records, and detecting potential documentation inconsistencies.

Every single qualification credit, license recommendation, and competency sign-off is committed and signed by a licensed, accountable human assessor.

Tough Field Conditions

Built for real-world conditions.

Assessment doesn’t happen in an air-conditioned lecture hall. Real trades happen on remote jobsites, industrial scaffolds, and regional agricultural fields where high-speed broadband does not exist.

SkillWorth’s Progressive Web Application operates completely offline. Candidates and evaluators can document hands-on work, record observations, and assign rubric ratings without internet connectivity.
